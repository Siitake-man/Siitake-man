## 📖 My Journey: From Traditional Networking to AI-Native Builder

For over a decade, I engineered **on-premises Cisco networks**, specializing deeply in IP routing. As I advanced into Technical Project Management (PM) overseeing upstream planning, I confronted a nagging vulnerability:  
*I felt a void in my core technical foundation across broader infrastructure — Linux internals, virtualization, modern middleware, and security architectures.*

**Then, I discovered the power of AI.**  
I realized that by pairing with AI to *actually build things*, I could absorb deep technical domains at exponential speed.

In **December 2025, discovering Google's Antigravity** ignited my journey into autonomous AI agents. Without formal mentors, I paved my own path: relentlessly experimenting, conversing with AI models as intellectual partners, researching bleeding-edge patterns from the global developer community, and embracing constant trial and error.

That relentless loop gave birth to **[Neo-Secretary (ネオ秘書くん)](https://github.com/Siitake-man/hisho-kun)** — an open-source zero-trust remote cockpit for autonomous coding agents.

Today, I feel my latent potential unlocking day by day, and I am genuinely thrilled about what lies ahead.  
**My ultimate goal is to evolve into a world-class AI Infrastructure Engineer.** I am committed to sharing every milestone, experiment, and architectural breakthrough openly here on GitHub.

## 🏛️ Engineering Philosophy

- 🔍 **First-Principles Architecture**: Never settle for surface-level "it works". Understand why electrons, packets, and tensors flow the way they do.
- 🛡️ **Zero-Trust by Default**: Fail-closed permissions, cryptographically verified boundaries, and strict client identity isolation.
- ✂️ **Subtraction & Essence**: Cut unnecessary bloat. Prioritize latency, battery efficiency (Adaptive Canvas 0fps), and user peace of mind.
- 少年 **Childlike Wonder & Absolute Positivism**: Build tools that bring a smile to your face — like a breathing retro 16-bit pixel companion cheering you on from your desk.
